Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Shinova

Which surfaces can I clean with Shinova home-cleaning products?

Shinova offers dedicated cleaners for floors, toilets, kitchens, glass, utensils and general household cleaning. Use each product only on the surface mentioned on its label.

How should I use Shinova Floor Cleaner?

Dilute the recommended quantity in water and mop the floor normally. For heavily soiled areas, use a slightly stronger solution as directed on the packaging.

Can Shinova Toilet Cleaner remove tough stains?

Yes. It is designed to clean toilet bowls, remove stubborn stains and help maintain everyday bathroom hygiene. Apply it under the rim, allow it to work and then scrub and flush.

Is Shinova Kitchen Cleaner suitable for greasy surfaces?

Yes. It is made for cleaning grease, food stains and everyday dirt from kitchen counters, stovetops, sinks and other washable kitchen surfaces.

Does Shinova Glass Cleaner leave streaks?

When used correctly with a clean microfiber cloth, it helps remove fingerprints, dust and marks while leaving glass and mirrors clean and shiny.

Can Shinova Dishwash Gel be used for daily utensil cleaning?

Yes. The dishwash gel is suitable for everyday cleaning of plates, glasses, cutlery, pots and pans. Use a small quantity on a wet sponge and rinse thoroughly.

What is White Phenyl used for?

White Phenyl is generally used for routine floor and surface cleaning in homes, offices, bathrooms and common areas. Dilute it according to the instructions before use.

Are these products safe to use around children and pets?

Use the products only as directed, keep them out of reach of children and pets, and avoid direct contact with eyes or skin. Do not mix different cleaning chemicals together.
